Overview

Task Planner is a local-first planning app. Your tasks, ideas, recurring items, tags, and history are stored on your device.

Getting Help

If you need help with Task Planner, email the developer at zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

You can also visit the Task Planner app page for product information and quick links.

When Reporting an Issue

Please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Task Planner version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Any steps that help reproduce the issue

Data Retention Note

Task Planner does not currently provide built-in backup export or import. Before reinstalling the app or clearing local app storage, note that existing local planner data may not be recoverable.
