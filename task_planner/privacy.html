Task Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Local-first tasks, local settings, no account required.

本機優先任務、裝置內設定，不需要帳號。

Core

Your planner data is stored on your device.

你的規劃資料會儲存在你的裝置上。

Network

No advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, push notifications, or account-based cloud sync currently used.

目前不使用廣告、追蹤、分析 SDK、推播通知，或以帳號為基礎的雲端同步。

01

Overview

Task Planner is a local-first task planning app designed for capturing tasks, ideas, recurring work, and completion history in one place.

Task Planner 是一款本機優先的任務規劃 app，用來把任務、想法、循環工作與完成紀錄收在同一個地方。

02

Data Storage

Task Planner stores your tasks, plan items, recurring items, tags, tag colors, completion history, dashboard summary data, and display settings on your device. Task Planner does not require an account.

Task Planner 會將你的任務、計畫項目、循環項目、標籤、標籤顏色、完成紀錄、儀表板摘要資料與顯示設定儲存在你的裝置上。Task Planner 不需要帳號。

03

Settings and Preferences

Task Planner may store local display preferences such as tag size and completed-task preview count. These preferences are stored only on your device as part of the local app state.

Task Planner 可能會儲存標籤大小、已完成任務預覽數量等本機顯示偏好。這些偏好只會作為本機 app 狀態的一部分儲存在你的裝置上。

04

Backups

Task Planner does not currently provide built-in backup export or import. If you delete the app or clear local app storage, your saved data may be lost.

Task Planner 目前不提供內建備份匯出或匯入。如果你刪除 app 或清除本機 app 儲存空間，已保存的資料可能會遺失。

05

Data Collection

Task Planner does not currently collect personal data. Task Planner does not currently use advertising, third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, push notifications, or account-based cloud sync.

Task Planner 目前不蒐集個人資料，也不使用廣告、第三方追蹤、分析 SDK、推播通知或以帳號為基礎的雲端同步。

06

Network Use

Task Planner is currently designed to work with local device storage. If future versions add backup, sync, analytics, or other network features, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

Task Planner 目前設計為使用裝置本機儲存。如果未來版本加入備份、同步、分析或其他網路功能，這份隱私權政策會在那些功能發布前更新。

07

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

如有支援或隱私相關問題，請聯絡 zis.tech.support@gmail.com。

08

Changes

This policy may be updated as Task Planner changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.

這份政策可能會隨 Task Planner 的變更而更新。政策更新時，上方的生效日期也會一併更新。
