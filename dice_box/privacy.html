Dice Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Local dice data, manual Collection backups, Apple-managed purchases, and banner advertising.

本機骰子資料、手動 Collection 備份、由 Apple 處理的購買，以及橫幅廣告。

On your device

Your Collection, Log, and preferences are stored on your device. Dice Box does not require an account.

Collection、Log 與設定儲存在你的裝置上，使用 Dice Box 不需要帳號。

Your choice

Collection backups are exported or imported only when you choose to do so. Dice Box does not automatically sync them.

只有在你主動選擇時，才會匯出或匯入 Collection 備份；Dice Box 不會自動同步。

01

Local storage

Dice Box saves your Decks, formulas and notes, roll history, and app preferences on your device. Preferences include language, appearance, selected dice color, and any color unlocks saved by an earlier test build. The Log records formulas, totals, and roll times. Dice Box does not require an account or provide automatic cloud sync.

Dice Box 會在裝置上保存 Deck、公式與備註、擲骰紀錄及 App 偏好設定。設定包含語言、外觀、選取的骰色，以及早期測試版留下的配色解鎖紀錄。Log 包含算式、總和與擲骰時間。Dice Box 不需要帳號，也沒有自動雲端同步。

To avoid repeated review invitations, Dice Box also stores first-use time, a small set of active dates, a completed-roll count, and the time and app version of its last review request on your device. These records are used locally to decide when to request Apple's native review prompt; Dice Box does not send them to an analytics service. Apple controls whether the prompt appears.

為避免重複邀請評分，Dice Box 也會在裝置上記錄首次使用時間、少量使用日期、完成擲骰的次數，以及上次請求評分的時間與 App 版本。這些紀錄僅在本機用於判斷何時請求 Apple 的原生評分提示，不會由 Dice Box 傳送至分析服務；提示是否顯示由 Apple 決定。

02

Collection backups

Dice Box Pro provides manual JSON export and import for Collection. An export contains Decks, saved formulas, and notes. It does not include Log, app settings, local color unlocks, or review-invitation records. The device share sheet lets you choose where to save or send the file. Dice Box does not automatically upload backups.

Dice Box Pro 提供 Collection 的手動 JSON 匯出與匯入。備份包含 Deck、收藏公式與備註，不含 Log、App 設定、本機配色解鎖紀錄或評分邀請紀錄。你可以透過裝置分享功能選擇檔案要存放或傳送的位置。Dice Box 不會自動上傳備份。

When you import a backup, Dice Box adds new Collection entries after you confirm. Existing entries with matching IDs stay unchanged.

匯入備份時，Dice Box 會在你確認後加入新收藏；ID 相同的既有收藏會保持原樣。

03

Purchases

Dice Box Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes banner ads, provides all eight dice colors, and enables Collection backup export and import. Purchases and Restore Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the purchase and its associated account information. Restoring Pro restores access to all dice colors.

Dice Box Pro 為選購的一次性買斷，包含移除橫幅廣告、使用全部八種骰色，以及 Collection 備份匯出／匯入。購買與回復購買使用 Apple 的 App 內購買系統，購買與相關帳號資訊由 Apple 處理。回復 Pro 可重新取得全部骰色的使用權。

04

Advertising and analytics

Dice Box uses Google AdMob to show banner ads in the free version. Store releases use live ads. Development builds use Google's test ads. Ad availability depends on your region, privacy choices, network connection, Google's approval of the app, and available ads. Dice Box does not include a separate analytics SDK.

Dice Box 免費版透過 Google AdMob 顯示橫幅廣告。商店版本使用正式廣告，開發版本使用 Google 測試廣告。廣告是否顯示取決於所在地區、隱私選擇、網路連線、Google 的 App 審核狀態及廣告供應。Dice Box 沒有另外加入分析 SDK。

When the advertising SDK is used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and advertising and interaction data to deliver, measure, and protect its advertising services. Test ads are also provided through Google's SDK and network. Dice Box does not send your Deck names, formulas or notes, roll history, or Collection backup files as ad targeting data.

使用廣告 SDK 時，Google 可能處理 IP 位址與概略位置、裝置或 App 識別碼、當機與效能資訊，以及廣告和互動資料，用於提供、衡量與保護廣告服務。測試廣告也會使用 Google 的 SDK 與網路。Dice Box 不會把 Deck 名稱、公式或備註、擲骰紀錄或 Collection 備份檔案作為廣告定位資料傳送。

In the free version, Dice Box uses Google's consent service and requests Apple's tracking permission before enabling banner ads. If you authorize tracking and your regional privacy choices allow ads, Dice Box requests non-personalized banner ads. Advertising partners may use your device identifier to measure ads across apps and websites. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings and, where required, revisit Ad privacy options in Dice Box Settings. Dice Box skips banner requests when tracking is unauthorized or the regional consent check blocks ads. Once Pro is confirmed, Dice Box removes the banner and stops new ad requests. Google may still retain data it previously processed.

免費版會先透過 Google 的同意服務確認地區隱私選擇，並在啟用橫幅廣告前要求 Apple 的追蹤權限。追蹤已授權且地區隱私選擇允許時，Dice Box 會請求非個人化橫幅廣告。廣告合作夥伴可能使用你的裝置識別碼，衡量跨 App 與網站的廣告成效。你可以在 iOS 設定變更追蹤權限，也可在適用時透過 Dice Box 設定的「廣告隱私選項」調整選擇。追蹤未獲授權或地區隱私檢查未通過時，Dice Box 會略過橫幅請求。確認 Pro 權益後，Dice Box 會移除橫幅並停止新的廣告請求。Google 仍可能保留先前已處理的資料。

For Google's data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google 的資料處理方式請參閱 Google 隱私權政策與 Google Mobile Ads 資料揭露說明。

05

Support, reviews, and external links

The Privacy Policy and Support links open this website. You can choose to email us from the Support page. More apps opens the developer's Apple App Store page, and Rate Dice Box opens Apple's review page. These links open only when you choose them. Apple handles ratings and reviews; Dice Box does not receive a confirmation of whether you submitted a rating or review.

「隱私權政策」和「支援」連結會開啟此網站；你可以從支援頁面選擇寄信給我們。「更多 App」會開啟開發者的 Apple App Store 頁面，「留下評分」則會開啟 Apple 的評論頁。這些連結只會在你主動點選時開啟。評分與評論由 Apple 處理，Dice Box 不會收到你是否已送出評分或評論的確認。

如有支援或隱私相關問題，請寄信至 zis.tech.support@gmail.com。此政策可能隨 Dice Box 的變更而更新，屆時也會更新生效日期。
