Dice Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Local dice data, manual Collection backups, and Apple-managed purchases.

本機骰子資料、手動 Collection 備份，以及由 Apple 處理的購買。

On your device

Your Collection, Log, and preferences are stored on your device. Dice Box does not require an account.

Collection、Log 與設定儲存在你的裝置上，使用 Dice Box 不需要帳號。

Your choice

Collection backups are exported or imported only when you choose to do so. Dice Box does not automatically sync them.

只有在你主動選擇時，才會匯出或匯入 Collection 備份；Dice Box 不會自動同步。

01

Local storage

Dice Box saves your roll history, saved formulas and notes, and app preferences on your device. The Log records formulas, totals, and roll times. Dice Box does not require an account or provide automatic cloud sync.

Dice Box 會在裝置上保存擲骰紀錄、收藏公式與備註，以及 App 設定。Log 包含算式、總和與擲骰時間。Dice Box 不需要帳號，也沒有自動雲端同步。

02

Collection backups

Dice Box Pro provides manual JSON export and import for Collection. An export contains saved formulas and notes, but not Log or settings. The device share sheet lets you choose where to save or send the file. Dice Box does not automatically upload backups.

Dice Box Pro 提供 Collection 的手動 JSON 匯出與匯入。備份包含收藏公式與備註，不含 Log 或設定。你可以透過裝置分享功能自行決定檔案要存放或傳送至何處；Dice Box 不會自動上傳備份。

When you import a backup, Dice Box adds new Collection entries after you confirm. Existing entries with matching IDs stay unchanged.

匯入備份時，Dice Box 會在你確認後加入新收藏；ID 相同的既有收藏會保持原樣。

03

Purchases

Dice Box Pro is an optional one-time purchase for removing banner ads and exporting or importing Collection backups. It is not a subscription. Purchases and Restore Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the purchase and its associated account information.

Dice Box Pro 為選購的一次性買斷，可移除橫幅廣告並匯出或匯入 Collection 備份，不是訂閱。購買與恢復購買會使用 Apple 的 App 內購買系統；購買與相關帳號資訊由 Apple 處理。

04

Advertising and analytics

Dice Box is preparing Google AdMob banner advertising. Development builds that include the Google Mobile Ads SDK may load Google's test banner ads; earlier builds only display a placement preview. Live advertising is not enabled yet. Dice Box does not include a separate analytics SDK.

Dice Box 正在準備 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告。包含 Google Mobile Ads SDK 的開發版可能載入 Google 測試橫幅廣告；較早的版本只顯示版位預覽。目前尚未啟用正式廣告。Dice Box 沒有另外加入分析 SDK。

When the advertising SDK is used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and advertising and interaction data to deliver, measure, and protect its advertising services. Test ads are also provided through Google's SDK and network. Dice Box does not send your Collection formulas or notes, roll history, or Collection backup files as ad targeting data.

使用廣告 SDK 時，Google 可能處理 IP 位址與概略位置、裝置或 App 識別碼、當機與效能資訊，以及廣告和互動資料，用於提供、衡量與保護廣告服務。測試廣告也會使用 Google 的 SDK 與網路。Dice Box 不會把 Collection 公式或備註、擲骰紀錄或 Collection 備份檔案作為廣告定位資料傳送。

Dice Box requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission. This does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. Before initializing ads, the app checks Google's consent status and displays a consent message when required. If ad requests are not permitted, no banner is loaded. Where required, you can revisit Ad privacy options in Settings. Once Pro is confirmed, the app removes the banner and stops requesting banner ads; this does not erase data previously processed by Google.

Dice Box 會請求非個人化廣告，不會要求 Apple 的追蹤權限；這不代表廣告 SDK 不會處理資料。啟動廣告前，App 會確認 Google 的同意狀態，並在需要時顯示同意訊息。如果不允許廣告請求，就不會載入橫幅。需要提供隱私選項時，你可以在 Settings 的 Ad privacy options 重新調整。確認 Pro 權益後，App 會移除橫幅並停止請求橫幅廣告，但不會刪除 Google 先前已處理的資料。

For Google's data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google 的資料處理方式請參閱 Google 隱私權政策與 Google Mobile Ads 資料揭露說明。

05

Support and external links

The Privacy Policy and Support links open this website. You can choose to email us from the Support page. The More apps link opens the developer's Apple App Store page. These actions happen only when you choose them.

「隱私權政策」和「支援」連結會開啟此網站；你可以從支援頁面選擇寄信給我們。「更多作品」會開啟開發者的 Apple App Store 頁面。這些動作只會在你主動點選時發生。

06

Contact and changes

For support or privacy questions, email zis.tech.support@gmail.com. This policy may change as Dice Box changes; its effective date will be updated when it does.

如有支援或隱私相關問題，請寄信至 zis.tech.support@gmail.com。此政策可能隨 Dice Box 的變更而更新，屆時也會更新生效日期。
