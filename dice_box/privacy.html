Policy

Dice Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Local dice data and color unlocks, manual Collection backups, Apple-managed purchases, and optional rewarded ads.

本機骰子資料與配色解鎖、手動 Collection 備份、由 Apple 處理的購買，以及自選獎勵廣告。

On your device

Your Collection, Log, and preferences are stored on your device. Dice Box does not require an account.

Collection、Log 與設定儲存在你的裝置上，使用 Dice Box 不需要帳號。

Your choice

Collection backups are exported or imported only when you choose to do so. Dice Box does not automatically sync them.

只有在你主動選擇時，才會匯出或匯入 Collection 備份；Dice Box 不會自動同步。

01

Local storage

Dice Box saves your Decks, formulas and notes, roll history, and app preferences on your device. Preferences include language, appearance, selected dice color, and colors unlocked through rewarded ads. The Log records formulas, totals, and roll times. Dice Box does not require an account or provide automatic cloud sync.

Dice Box 會在裝置上保存 Deck、公式與備註、擲骰紀錄及 App 偏好設定。設定包含語言、外觀、選取的骰色，以及透過獎勵廣告解鎖的顏色。Log 包含算式、總和與擲骰時間。Dice Box 不需要帳號，也沒有自動雲端同步。

To avoid repeated review invitations, Dice Box also stores first-use time, a small set of active dates, a completed-roll count, and the time and app version of its last review request on your device. These records are used locally to decide when to request Apple's native review prompt; Dice Box does not send them to an analytics service. Apple controls whether the prompt appears.

為避免重複邀請評分，Dice Box 也會在裝置上記錄首次使用時間、少量使用日期、完成擲骰的次數，以及上次請求評分的時間與 App 版本。這些紀錄僅在本機用於判斷何時請求 Apple 的原生評分提示，不會由 Dice Box 傳送至分析服務；提示是否顯示由 Apple 決定。

02

Collection backups

Dice Box Pro provides manual JSON export and import for Collection. An export contains Decks, saved formulas, and notes. It does not include Log, app settings, rewarded-ad color unlocks, or review-invitation records. The device share sheet lets you choose where to save or send the file. Dice Box does not automatically upload backups.

Dice Box Pro 提供 Collection 的手動 JSON 匯出與匯入。備份包含 Deck、收藏公式與備註，不含 Log、App 設定、獎勵廣告的配色解鎖狀態或評分邀請紀錄。你可以透過裝置分享功能自行決定檔案要存放或傳送至何處；Dice Box 不會自動上傳備份。

When you import a backup, Dice Box adds new Collection entries after you confirm. Existing entries with matching IDs stay unchanged.

匯入備份時，Dice Box 會在你確認後加入新收藏；ID 相同的既有收藏會保持原樣。

03

Purchases

Dice Box Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes banner ads, provides all eight dice colors without watching ads, and enables Collection backup export and import. It is not a subscription. Purchases and Restore Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the purchase and its associated account information. Restoring Pro restores access to all dice colors; it does not restore local ad-earned unlock records.

Dice Box Pro 為選購的一次性買斷，包含移除橫幅廣告、免看廣告使用全部八種骰色，以及 Collection 備份匯出／匯入，不是訂閱。購買與回復購買使用 Apple 的 App 內購買系統；購買與相關帳號資訊由 Apple 處理。回復 Pro 可重新取得全部骰色的使用權，不會還原本機的廣告解鎖紀錄。

04

Advertising and analytics

Dice Box integrates Google AdMob for banner advertising and optional rewarded ads that unlock selected dice colors. Store releases are configured for live ads; development builds use Google's test ads. Ad availability depends on your region, privacy choices, network connection, and Google's app approval and ad supply. Dice Box does not include a separate analytics SDK.

Dice Box 整合 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告，以及可選擇觀看、用來解鎖指定骰色的獎勵廣告。商店版本設定為正式廣告，開發版本使用 Google 測試廣告；能否顯示廣告取決於所在地區、隱私選擇、網路連線，以及 Google 的 App 核准狀態與廣告供應。Dice Box 沒有另外加入分析 SDK。

Rewarded ads start only when you choose Watch ad to unlock. A color unlocks after the ad service confirms that the reward was earned. Closing an ad without earning the reward, or an unavailable ad, does not unlock the color. The unlock is stored on this installation and is not included in Collection backups; reinstalling the app or changing devices does not restore these ad-earned unlocks. Pro provides all colors without requiring rewarded ads.

獎勵廣告只會在你主動選擇「觀看廣告解鎖」後啟動，並在廣告服務確認已取得獎勵後，解鎖選定顏色。未取得獎勵就關閉廣告，或廣告無法載入，都不會解鎖。解鎖狀態保存在目前安裝的 App 中，不包含在 Collection 備份；重新安裝或更換裝置不會還原這些廣告解鎖狀態。Pro 可免看獎勵廣告使用全部配色。

When the advertising SDK is used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and advertising and interaction data to deliver, measure, and protect its advertising services. Test ads are also provided through Google's SDK and network. Dice Box does not send your Deck names, formulas or notes, roll history, or Collection backup files as ad targeting data.

使用廣告 SDK 時，Google 可能處理 IP 位址與概略位置、裝置或 App 識別碼、當機與效能資訊，以及廣告和互動資料，用於提供、衡量與保護廣告服務。測試廣告也會使用 Google 的 SDK 與網路。Dice Box 不會把 Deck 名稱、公式或備註、擲骰紀錄或 Collection 備份檔案作為廣告定位資料傳送。

Dice Box requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission. This does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. Before initializing ads, the app checks Google's consent status and displays a consent message when required. If ad requests are not permitted, neither banner nor rewarded ads are requested. Where required, you can revisit Ad privacy options in Settings. Once Pro is confirmed, the app removes the banner, provides every dice color, and does not initiate new banner or rewarded ad requests; this does not erase data previously processed by Google.

Dice Box 會請求非個人化廣告，不會要求 Apple 的追蹤權限；這不代表廣告 SDK 不會處理資料。啟動廣告前，App 會確認 Google 的同意狀態，並在需要時顯示同意訊息。如果不允許廣告請求，就不會請求橫幅或獎勵廣告。需要提供隱私選項時，你可以在設定中的廣告隱私選項重新調整。確認 Pro 權益後，App 會移除橫幅、提供全部骰色，並停止發起新的橫幅或獎勵廣告請求，但不會刪除 Google 先前已處理的資料。

For Google's data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google 的資料處理方式請參閱 Google 隱私權政策與 Google Mobile Ads 資料揭露說明。

05

Support, reviews, and external links

The Privacy Policy and Support links open this website. You can choose to email us from the Support page. More apps opens the developer's Apple App Store page, and Rate Dice Box opens Apple's review page. These links open only when you choose them. Apple handles ratings and reviews; Dice Box does not receive a confirmation of whether you submitted a rating or review.

「隱私權政策」和「支援」連結會開啟此網站；你可以從支援頁面選擇寄信給我們。「更多 App」會開啟開發者的 Apple App Store 頁面，「留下評分」則會開啟 Apple 的評論頁。這些連結只會在你主動點選時開啟。評分與評論由 Apple 處理，Dice Box 不會收到你是否已送出評分或評論的確認。

如有支援或隱私相關問題，請寄信至 zis.tech.support@gmail.com。此政策可能隨 Dice Box 的變更而更新，屆時也會更新生效日期。
