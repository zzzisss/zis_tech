Last updated: 2026-06-25

This guide is kept as a design-direction reference for the shared `zis tech` brand identity.
Use it when creating app splash screens, website headers, App Store images,
social graphics, portfolio pages, GitHub README sections, and public product pages.

---

## Brand Core

`zis tech` is a personal product brand for everyday apps, tools, and small systems.

It should feel:

- calm
- precise
- thoughtful
- lightly celestial
- close to everyday use
- useful without feeling noisy

The brand should not feel overly futuristic, overly cute, corporate-generic, or decorative.

---

## Primary Identity

### Primary Wordmark

Use:

```text
zis tech
```

Recommended usage:

- website header
- app splash screen
- GitHub README
- portfolio page
- App Store promotional image corner signature
- public brand or product page

Style guidance:

- Prefer dark charcoal on warm off-white or light neutral backgrounds.
- Keep generous spacing around the wordmark.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, or heavy effects.
- Do not compress letter spacing until it feels crowded.
- Let the wordmark feel calm and deliberate.

### Core Symbol

Use the original `2 / Orbit Mark` from the selected brand exploration.

Internal name:

```text
Orbit Mark
```

Meaning:

```text
A celestial core shaped by letters, orbit, and gravity.
```

Traditional Chinese meaning:

```text
以字母、軌道與引力構成的星體核心。
```

Recommended usage:

- social avatar
- favicon
- app splash symbol
- website corner mark
- App Store image signature
- product family mark
- compact brand stamp

Style guidance:

- Use `Orbit Mark` when space is too small for the full `zis tech` wordmark.
- Keep enough breathing room around it, especially in circular crops.
- Do not stretch, squash, rotate, or redraw it casually.
- Do not add extra stars, extra orbits, or decorative backgrounds.
- The mark already contains the celestial idea; let it stay quiet.

## Brand Roles

### `zis tech`

The formal brand signature.

Use it when the viewer should recognize the maker or product family.

Good contexts:

- public website
- product page header
- README title
- App Store image footer
- portfolio cover

### `Orbit Mark`

The compact brand core.

Use it when the full name would be too large or too word-heavy.

Good contexts:

- favicon
- social avatar
- app splash symbol
- small product corner mark
- visual watermark

### Brand Line

The positioning phrase.

Use it when the viewer needs a little more meaning than the logo alone provides.

Good contexts:

- website hero support line
- README banner
- About page
- brand identity sheet

## Product Family Fit

Current product examples:

- `Spark Note`: calm, local-first note space for ideas and daily fragments

- Future apps: should be allowed to feel more lively, useful, or playful while still returning to the same brand core

The shared brand should remain stable, while individual apps can have their own personality.

---

## Do

- Keep layouts spacious and precise.
- Use restrained colors.
- Let the symbol and wordmark breathe.
- Use `Orbit Mark` for small surfaces.
- Use the wordmark for formal brand recognition.
- Keep the celestial idea subtle rather than literal.

## Do Not

- Do not use loud gradients or glow effects.
- Do not turn the mark into a literal cartoon planet.
- Do not add extra rings, stars, or space decorations around the mark.
- Do not distort the wordmark or `Orbit Mark`.
- Do not use overly rounded, bubbly, or playful typography for the parent brand.
- Do not make every app recreate the parent brand from scratch.
