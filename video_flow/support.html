Video Flow Support

Current version: 1.0.0

Overview

Video Flow is a local-first app for organizing YouTube links. Your categories, saved links, titles, and notes are stored on your device, and manual backup files are controlled by you.

Getting Help

If you need help with Video Flow, email the developer at zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

You can also visit the Video Flow app page for product information and quick links.

When Reporting an Issue

Please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Video Flow version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Any steps that help reproduce the issue

YouTube Links

Video Flow saves and organizes YouTube links, then opens those links in YouTube or your browser. It does not download, save offline copies of, or extract audio from YouTube videos.

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Video Flow settings if you want to keep your categories and saved links.

Privacy

Read the Video Flow Privacy Policy.
