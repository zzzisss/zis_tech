01

Overview

Video Flow is a local-first app for organizing saved YouTube links into personal categories.

Video Flow 是一款本機優先的 app，用來把保存的 YouTube 連結整理到個人分類中。

02

Getting Help

If you need help with Video Flow, email the developer at zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

如果你需要 Video Flow 的協助，請寄信給開發者：zis.tech.support@gmail.com。

You can also visit the Video Flow app page for product information and quick links.

你也可以前往 Video Flow app 頁面 查看產品資訊與快速連結。

03

When Reporting an Issue

Please include:

回報問題時，請盡量提供以下資訊：

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Video Flow version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Any steps that help reproduce the issue
04

YouTube Note

Video Flow may use network access for YouTube title lookup, thumbnail loading, and opening YouTube links in YouTube or your browser.

Video Flow 可能會使用網路來查詢 YouTube 標題、載入縮圖，以及在 YouTube 或瀏覽器中開啟 YouTube 連結。

05

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Video Flow settings if you want to keep your saved links and notes.

如果你想保留保存的連結與筆記，重新安裝 app 或更換裝置前，請先從 Video Flow 設定中匯出 JSON 備份。
