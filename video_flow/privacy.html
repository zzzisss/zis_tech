Video Flow Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Overview Privacy Support

Overview

Video Flow is a local-first app for saving and organizing YouTube links into personal categories with notes.

Data Storage

Video Flow stores your categories, saved YouTube links, video titles, notes, item order, trash state, and backup-related data on your device. Video Flow does not require an account.

YouTube Links

When you add a YouTube link, Video Flow may request public video title information from YouTube oEmbed to reduce manual typing. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube image URLs. When you open a saved video, Video Flow opens the original YouTube URL in YouTube or your browser.

Video Flow does not download, cache, extract, or redistribute YouTube video or audio content.

Backups

Video Flow supports manual JSON backup export and import. Backup files are created only when you choose to export them. You are responsible for where exported backup files are saved or shared.

Importing a backup replaces the current local app data only after you confirm the import.

Data Collection

Video Flow does not currently collect personal data. Video Flow does not currently use advertising, third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, push notifications, or account-based cloud sync.

Network Use

Network access is used for YouTube title lookup, YouTube thumbnail loading, and opening YouTube links. If future versions add sync, cloud backup, analytics, or other network features, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Video Flow changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.
