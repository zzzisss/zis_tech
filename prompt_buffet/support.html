01

Overview

Prompt Buffet is a local-first prompt app. Your prompts remain on your device by default, and manual backups stay under your control. Prompt Buffet Pro in version 1.2.0 and later adds optional private iCloud sync and special themes.

Prompt Buffet 是一款本機優先的 prompt app。資料預設留在裝置中，手動備份檔也由你自行管理；1.2.0 之後的 Prompt Buffet Pro 則加入可選擇的私人 iCloud 同步與特殊主題。

04

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Prompt Buffet settings if you want to keep your prompts.

如果你想保留 prompt，重新安裝 app 或更換裝置前，請先從 Prompt Buffet 設定中匯出 JSON 備份。

05

Prompt Buffet Pro

Prompt Buffet Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks optional private iCloud sync and dot or grid themes. Core local features, including prompt creation, categories, search, unlimited pins, and manual backups, remain free.

Prompt Buffet Pro 是一次買斷商品，可解鎖自行選擇的私人 iCloud 同步，以及點點或方格特殊主題。新增 prompt、分類、搜尋、無限釘選與手動備份等本機核心功能仍可免費使用。

If a purchase is not recognized, open Settings > Prompt Buffet Pro and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

若購買狀態沒有恢復，請使用原購買所用的 Apple 帳號，前往「設定 > Prompt Buffet Pro」並選擇「恢復購買」。

06

iCloud Sync

Buying or restoring Pro does not enable sync automatically. When enabled, Prompt Buffet merges the local library with private iCloud data for the current iCloud account. Turning sync off stops future synchronization on that device and does not delete local prompts.

購買或恢復 Pro 不會自動開啟同步。主動開啟後，Prompt Buffet 會合併本機資料庫與目前 iCloud 帳號的私人資料；關閉同步會停止該裝置後續同步，但不會刪除本機 prompt。
