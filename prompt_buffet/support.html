Overview

Prompt Buffet is a local-first prompt app. Your prompts, categories, and manual backups are controlled by you.

Getting Help

If you need help with Prompt Buffet, email the developer at zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

You can also visit the Prompt Buffet app page for product information and quick links.

When Reporting an Issue

Please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Prompt Buffet version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Any steps that help reproduce the issue

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Prompt Buffet settings if you want to keep your prompts.

Privacy

Read the Prompt Buffet Privacy Policy.
