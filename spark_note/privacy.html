Spark Note Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Local-first notes, optional private iCloud sync, no account required.

本機優先筆記、可選擇私人 iCloud 同步，不需要帳號。

Core

Your notes and app data stay on your device unless you choose to enable Pro iCloud sync.

除非你選擇開啟 Pro iCloud 同步，筆記與 app 資料都會留在你的裝置上。

Network

No advertising or tracking. RevenueCat validates Pro purchases, and optional sync uses your private iCloud database.

沒有廣告或追蹤。RevenueCat 用於驗證 Pro 購買；選用的同步功能使用你的私人 iCloud 資料庫。

01

Overview

Spark Note is a local-first note app designed for quick capture, organization, and reflection.

Spark Note 是一款本機優先的筆記 app，設計給快速記錄、整理與回看使用。

02

Data Storage

Spark Note stores your notes, boxes, settings, trash, theme preference, and Spark usage data on your device. Spark Note does not require an account.

Spark Note 會將你的筆記、分類箱、設定、垃圾桶、主題偏好與 Spark 使用資料儲存在你的裝置上。Spark Note 不需要帳號。

03

Optional iCloud Sync

After unlocking Pro, you may choose to sync notes, favorites, boxes, and trash through your private Apple iCloud CloudKit database. Sync is off by default and can be turned off at any time. Appearance settings, Spark history, and purchase status remain device-local.

解鎖 Pro 後，你可以自行選擇透過 Apple iCloud CloudKit 的私人資料庫同步筆記、收藏、分類箱與垃圾桶。同步預設關閉，也能隨時關閉；外觀設定、Spark 紀錄與購買狀態仍保留在裝置本機。

04

Purchases and RevenueCat

Spark Note uses RevenueCat to validate the optional non-consumable Pro purchase and provide the Pro entitlement. RevenueCat receives an automatically generated anonymous app user ID and purchase or entitlement information for app functionality and purchase analytics. Spark Note does not provide RevenueCat with a custom user ID or use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Spark Note 使用 RevenueCat 驗證選購的非消耗型 Pro 購買並提供 Pro 權限。RevenueCat 會取得自動產生的匿名 app 使用者 ID，以及購買或權限資訊，用於 app 功能與購買分析。Spark Note 不會提供自訂使用者 ID，也不會將這些資訊用於廣告或跨 app 追蹤。

05

Backups and Update Checks

Spark Note supports user-initiated JSON backup export and import. Backup files are created only when you choose to export them, and you control where they are saved or shared. The app may also fetch a small public version file to check whether an update is available.

Spark Note 支援由你主動執行的 JSON 備份匯出與匯入。只有在你選擇匯出時才會建立備份檔，儲存或分享位置也由你自行決定。App 也可能讀取一份小型公開版本檔案，以檢查是否有可用更新。

06

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

如有支援或隱私相關問題，請聯絡 zis.tech.support@gmail.com。

07

Changes

This policy may be updated as Spark Note changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.

這份政策可能會隨 Spark Note 的變更而更新。政策更新時，上方的生效日期也會一併更新。
