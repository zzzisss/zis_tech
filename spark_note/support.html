01

Overview

Spark Note is a local-first note app. Starting with version 1.1.4, notes remain on your device unless you unlock Pro and explicitly enable private iCloud sync. Manual backup files remain controlled by you.

Spark Note 是一款本機優先的筆記 app。從 1.1.4 版起，除非你解鎖 Pro 並自行開啟私人 iCloud 同步，筆記都會儲存在裝置上；手動備份檔仍由你自行管理。

04

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Spark Note settings if you want to keep your notes.

如果你想保留筆記，重新安裝 app 或更換裝置前，請先從 Spark Note 設定中匯出 JSON 備份。

05

Pro Purchase and iCloud Sync

In version 1.1.4 or later, if a previous Pro purchase is not recognized, open Spark Note settings, enter the Pro page, and choose Restore Purchases. iCloud sync requires an active iCloud account and remains off until you enable it.

在 1.1.4 或後續版本中，如果先前購買的 Pro 沒有被辨識，請開啟 Spark Note 設定、進入 Pro 頁面並選擇「恢復購買」。iCloud 同步需要有效的 iCloud 帳號，並會維持關閉直到你自行開啟。
