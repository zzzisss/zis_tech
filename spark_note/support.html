Spark Note Support

Current version: 1.0.0

Overview

Spark Note is a local-first note app. Your notes are stored on your device, and manual backup files are controlled by you.

Getting Help

If you need help with Spark Note, email the developer at zis.tech.support@gmail.com.

You can also visit the Spark Note app page for product information and quick links.

When Reporting an Issue

Please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Spark Note version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Any steps that help reproduce the issue

Backups

Before reinstalling the app or replacing your device, export a JSON backup from Spark Note settings if you want to keep your notes.

Privacy

Read the Spark Note Privacy Policy.
